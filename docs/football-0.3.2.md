# Football 0.3.2 / Hub 0.1.21

Right mouse input is a single contextual action interpreted by the host: pass with possession, tackle without it, or take a restart. Keyboard P and E remain separate. Team control follows every new outfield ball owner, including AI recoveries and goalkeeper distributions. Fixed-player control keeps its selected position. The quick-match adapter assigns an unclaimed owner only to a team-mode controller, without taking a player already used by another connected controller.

Standing tackles can reach an exposed ball before players' bodies collide. DEF still affects reach and cooldown. Rear contact without winning the ball first can still be a foul; goalkeeper protection and evidence-based VAR remain. AI teammates use the same rules.

Quick match defaults to 11v11 and also offers the previous 5v5 format. The host publishes the format through lobby presence and the start packet; snapshots support 10 or 22 players. Both formats retain two halves, substitutions, goalkeeper AI, offside, restarts and recorded replays. New fictional quick-match players do not affect Ultimate Club cards or the wallet.

Offside uses the ball position at the moment of the kick and the second-last defender, in either direction and either half. Being behind the ball, level with the line or in one's own half is onside. A direct free kick and a keeper's ordinary pass can produce offside. Direct throw-ins, goal kicks and corners are exempt. An offside position is penalized only when the flagged player touches the ball in this 2D simulation.

The launcher theme surface now fills and clips to the whole app window. Artwork is inside the shell, with coordinated sidebar, header, friends, content and card palettes. It is no longer painted on the body outside the rounded app. Settings show all four free and nine account-owned bundles, with locked choices remaining disabled. Equipping a launcher theme restores its matching accent; a custom accent can still be selected afterwards. Purchases and ownership are checked by the existing server RPC. Three additional bundles are Stadium night (450 ZC), Neon city (600 ZC) and Winter hush (500 ZC).

Future completed playtime minutes earn 5 ZC instead of 30. The upgrade replaces only the existing private wallet view/reward functions, adds the new bundle rows and expands the two theme checks. Existing balances, paid minutes, wallet receipts, purchases, inventories and RPC routing are preserved. Historical 30 ZC rewards are not recalculated. The wallet UI says 5 ZC.

The Football cover and locker-room background use a new stadium illustration, embedded in a self-contained SVG with the original title treatment. Generated with built-in imagegen; the project asset is `assets/football-cover.svg`. Prompt: polished wide football game key art, emerald pitch at night, fictional adult player in a plain mint kit preparing a shot on the right, ball in foreground, crowd and floodlights, dark navy/teal palette and calm dark left side for the app title; no lettering, logos, sponsors or real clubs. The original generated PNG remains separate from the optimized game asset.

Validation covers contextual input, clean front tackles, owner switching and fixed-player preservation; offside geometry for 5v5/11v11, either team and half; free-kick/exempt restart behavior; past 30 ZC rewards followed by once-only 5 ZC rewards; locked and purchased themes; actual desktop 5v5 and 11v11, two SDK clients, Ultimate Club, themed window bounds and responsive controls. The Windows workflow verifies an installed upgrade from public Hub 0.1.20.
